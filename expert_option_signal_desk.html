Method and limitations
• Hourly trend is calculated from completed 1-hour candles; entry indicators use completed 1-minute candles.
• EMA 9/21, RSI 14, MACD 12/26/9 and ATR 14 are used for entry analysis.
• Live tick momentum confirms short-term direction before a signal is issued.
• Each signal is evaluated after its selected expiry using Deriv's live quote stream. Broker quotes, payout, spread, execution delay and platform-specific settlement may differ.
• A signal score is not a probability and cannot guarantee a profitable trade. Use this as a signal-only research tool.
